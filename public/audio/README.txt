Put your own copy of the song here, named dynamite.mp3 (or dynamite.m4a, .aac, .ogg or .wav).
The BTS stage show in the garden plays it, looped, when you press "Play Dynamite".

For the MCR stage show at the Concert Grounds, put your own copy of "Welcome to the Black Parade" here
named black-parade.mp3 (or black-parade.m4a, .aac, .ogg or .wav; mcr.mp3 also works).
It plays, looped, when you press "Play Black Parade". Without it, the game's own rock march plays instead.
